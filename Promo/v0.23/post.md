## EN

```
Project Builder: Roguelike – new version 0.23: weapons!

Build a house stage by stage, fighting construction problems: leaks, short circuits, mould and the Hard Deadline.
Now you fight them with tools that really feel different.

What's new:
- 45 weapons: a default one for each of the 12 trades, 25 to find on site and 8 artifacts
- hit types: Blunt stuns, Cut crits more often, Stab ignores armour – and the problem's material (cement, steel, wood) changes the damage
- rarity: Basic, Sturdy, Branded, Master – you can see it in your hero's hands and on the ground
- Trick: every weapon has its own trade move with a cooldown (hold the button to read it)
- random weapon perks and the Rigger Holster Belt for a second weapon
- artifacts with their own mechanics, e.g. the Thunder Maul – lightning every 5th hit – collected in your Showcase
- the legendary Senior Foreman's Knife: "The blade begs to retire. The foreman: no!"
- gear (helmet, gloves, vest) shows on your hero
- punchier announcements, a tougher Normal and rarer crits
- problems path around obstacles, side passages are easier to read, and the tutorial is shorter with bigger text

Easter egg: sometimes CoffeeDog, the site dog, sits in the mud. Pet her and she'll rate your progress, brutally honestly
("New on site? Rule one: don't step in wet concrete."). Pet her in 4 builds for the Dog's Best Pal badge.

Even if the fumes get to you – you can still play.

On iPhone, Android and as a retro version. A game from the maker of PlanBudowlany: planbudowlany.online
```
